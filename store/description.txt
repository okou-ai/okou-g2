Talk to Okou from your G2 glasses. Tap the temple to record a voice message, then tap again to send it to a dedicated conversation in your Okou workspace. Read the latest reply on the lens and swipe through longer answers, even while recording. Hold the temple while recording to discard a clip.

To get started, sign in to your Okou account on your phone and choose a workspace. Sending recordings and receiving replies requires an internet connection.
